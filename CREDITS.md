# Credits — The Classic+ Multiverse & Omniverse

This project is a **fan-made Modding Tree mod** that ports and reimagines other prestige-tree games into a unified Multiverse and Omniverse framework. All original games remain property of their creators — this mod exists to celebrate them and to show how TMT can hold *every* tree in one cohesive universe.

## Ported Games — The 12 Multiverse Realms (Universe Layer `U`)

We `git cloned` the originals into `/tmp` and adapt their formulas into dedicated universe sub-tabs and buyables:

### 1. Universe C — Prestige Tree **Classic** (1.0)
- **Original:** `Jacorb90/Prestige-Tree-Classic` — https://github.com/Jacorb90/Prestige-Tree-Classic
- **Author:** **Jacorb90** (with help by Aarex, idea by papyrus)
- **Cloned to:** `/tmp/PT-Classic` (7 rows, 22 layers, 7889 lines)
- **Ported:** P/B/G buyables (Prestige, Boosters, Generators) with original formulas.
